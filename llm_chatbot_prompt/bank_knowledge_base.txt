SecureBank is a modern digital banking institution established in 2010, headquartered in San Francisco, California. We serve over 2 million customers across the United States with a commitment to secure, convenient, and innovative banking solutions.

Our main headquarters is located at 450 Market Street, San Francisco, CA 94111. We operate 150 branch locations nationwide, with our largest presence in California, New York, Texas, Florida, and Illinois. All branches are open Monday through Friday from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, and Saturday from 9:00 AM to 1:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and federal holidays.

SecureBank offers three types of checking accounts: Basic Checking with no monthly fee and no minimum balance requirement, Premium Checking with a $15 monthly fee (waived with $5,000 minimum balance) that includes free checks and cashback rewards, and Student Checking for customers aged 18-24 with no fees and free ATM withdrawals nationwide.

Our savings accounts include a Standard Savings Account with 0.50% APY and no monthly fee if you maintain a $300 minimum balance, and a High-Yield Savings Account offering 4.25% APY with a $10,000 minimum balance requirement. Interest on all savings accounts is compounded daily and paid monthly.

We provide personal loans ranging from $1,000 to $50,000 with interest rates starting at 6.99% APR for qualified borrowers. Loan terms are available from 12 to 84 months. Our home mortgage rates start at 6.25% for a 30-year fixed mortgage and 5.75% for a 15-year fixed mortgage, with down payment requirements as low as 3% for first-time homebuyers.

SecureBank credit cards include the SecureCard Classic with no annual fee and 1% cashback on all purchases, the SecureCard Gold with a $95 annual fee offering 2% cashback on groceries and gas and 1% on everything else, and the SecureCard Premium with a $195 annual fee providing 3% cashback on travel and dining, 2% on groceries, and 1% on other purchases, plus travel insurance and airport lounge access.

Our ATM network includes over 40,000 fee-free ATMs nationwide through our partnership with the Allpoint network. Using out-of-network ATMs incurs a $3.50 fee per transaction. We reimburse up to $20 in ATM fees monthly for Premium Checking account holders.

Mobile banking is available through our SecureBank Mobile App, which supports mobile check deposit with limits of $5,000 per day and $10,000 per month, instant person-to-person payments using Zelle, fingerprint and face recognition login, and real-time transaction alerts. The app is available for both iOS and Android devices.

Wire transfer services are available with domestic wires costing $25 for outgoing transfers and international wires costing $45. Incoming domestic wires are free, while incoming international wires cost $15. Wire transfers typically process within 1 business day for domestic transfers and 2-3 business days for international transfers.

Our customer service is available 24/7 by phone at 1-800-BANK-HELP (1-800-226-5435). Live chat support is available through our website and mobile app from 6:00 AM to midnight Pacific Time daily. Email support typically responds within 24 hours during business days.

SecureBank maintains FDIC insurance coverage up to $250,000 per depositor, per account ownership category. We also offer additional protection through our partnership with Massachusetts Bankers Association Depositors Insurance Fund for amounts exceeding FDIC limits.

Overdraft protection is available through linking to a savings account (no fee), linking to a credit card ($10 fee per transfer), or through our courtesy overdraft service which charges $35 per overdraft item up to 4 times per day. You can opt out of overdraft services at any time.

Our business banking services include business checking accounts with no monthly fees for balances over $10,000, merchant services with competitive credit card processing rates starting at 2.5% plus $0.10 per transaction, and business loans up to $500,000 with SBA loan options available.

We offer certificates of deposit (CDs) with terms ranging from 3 months to 5 years. Current rates are 4.5% APY for a 1-year CD with a $1,000 minimum deposit, 4.75% APY for a 3-year CD, and 5.0% APY for a 5-year CD. Early withdrawal penalties apply: 90 days of interest for CDs under 1 year, 180 days for 1-3 year CDs, and 365 days for CDs over 3 years.

SecureBank uses state-of-the-art encryption and security measures including 256-bit SSL encryption for all online transactions, two-factor authentication for account access, real-time fraud monitoring, and zero liability protection for unauthorized transactions reported within 60 days.

Our rewards program, SecurePoints, allows customers to earn 1 point per dollar spent on debit card purchases. Points can be redeemed for cashback at a rate of 10,000 points = $100, or for travel, gift cards, and merchandise through our rewards portal. Points never expire as long as the account remains active.

For foreign currency exchange, we offer competitive rates with no commission fees for amounts over $1,000. Orders can be placed online or at major branch locations and are typically ready for pickup within 2-3 business days. We support over 50 currencies including Euro, British Pound, Japanese Yen, Canadian Dollar, and Australian Dollar.

Investment services are available through SecureBank Investment Services, a separate entity, offering brokerage accounts, retirement planning, mutual funds, and financial advisory services. A dedicated financial advisor is complimentary for customers with combined balances exceeding $100,000.

Our online bill pay service is free for all checking account customers and allows you to schedule one-time or recurring payments to anyone in the United States. Payments to enrolled billers typically arrive within 1-2 business days, while payments by check can take 5-7 business days.

SecureBank participates in the Early Direct Deposit program, allowing customers to receive their paycheck up to 2 days earlier than their scheduled pay date at no additional cost. This feature is automatically available to all checking account holders who set up direct deposit.

Identity theft protection services are offered in partnership with IdentityGuard for $9.99 per month, including credit monitoring from all three bureaus, dark web monitoring, up to $1 million in identity theft insurance, and dedicated fraud resolution specialists available 24/7.
